## Indian Military Airworthiness Procedure

The [DRDO certification guidance index](https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/en/documents/form-and-manual/airworthiness-certification-guidelines-documents) lists IMAP 2023. The [published IMAP 2023](https://drdo.gov.in/drdo/sites/default/files/form_formats/IMAP2023Latest_0.pdf) covers rig approval for development and production in §1.4.11 and airborne-store testing in §1.5.8. Both provisions allow eligible non-critical self-approval of test plans and procedures. §1.5.4 identifies the three airborne-store criticality levels through a system safety assessment at air system level. The page wording reflects those sections.

Original Section 7 bullets:

> Test rigs are themselves subject to approval. Under that procedure, the technical specifications of test rigs used during development and production for verification and validation of an air system or airborne store are approved by CEMILAC, and the rigs are certified by CEMILAC or DGAQA. Test plans and procedure documents are likewise reviewed and approved. For anyone specifying a bench for Indian military work, this reframes the purchase: the rig is a certifiable item, not just capital equipment.

> Criticality drives the extent of testing. Components are classified by criticality — flight safety critical, where failure endangers the aircraft or crew, through major and minor to non-critical — with the classification decided by the Local Type Certification Committee. The approach to certification and type approval, including how much testing is required, follows from that classification. A bench for a flight safety critical component carries a heavier burden than one for a minor item.

Original FAQ 8 answer:

> There is no single workmanship standard for test benches. What governs one is the test specification it must satisfy, the quality system of the organisation operating it, and the traceability of its measurements. For Indian military programmes, qualification and acceptance requirements sit within the Indian Military Airworthiness Procedure — successor to DDPMAS — under which the technical specifications of test rigs are approved by CEMILAC and the rigs certified by CEMILAC or DGAQA.
