The draft omits the controlled-airspace condition and names only Appendix E. The rule applies to IFR flight in controlled airspace and refers to Appendices E and F. [14 CFR § 91.411(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-91/subpart-E/section-91.411)
